What does Toby Studio do?+

Toby Studio is the independent design studio of Tobiloba Julius Adelani, also known online as Julious Design. I design websites, landing pages, mobile apps and product interfaces (UI/UX), and I build launch-ready websites with AI when you need to go live fast.

Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?+

Yes. I'm based in Ibadan, Nigeria and work remotely with founders, startups and brands worldwide. Feedback, calls and handoff all happen online.

How much does a website or app design cost?+

Every project is scoped individually, because a single landing page and a full product are very different jobs. Share your goals, timeline and budget range, and I'll send a clear proposal with pricing before any work starts.

How long does a project take?+

It depends on scope. A focused landing page moves much faster than a multi-screen app. You'll get a realistic timeline upfront, and regular updates as we go.

Can you build the website too, or only design it?+

Both. I can hand your developers clean, developer-ready designs, or design and build the website with AI-powered tools so it's live and ready for customers.

What do you need from me to get started?+

A short brief: what you do, who it's for and what you want visitors to do. Examples of sites you like help too. Send it by email or WhatsApp and we'll take it from there.
